# NBS World: Cyberpunk

<skill name="setting" parent="narration" active="cyberpunk">
Setting: cyberpunk.

Attitudes. Everyone is for sale and knows it. Cynicism is baseline and sincerity is suspicious. Nobody expects justice, only leverage. The body is equipment - upgraded, financed, repossessed. Nostalgia sells; the past is a texture, not a memory.

Hierarchy. Corporations are the real sovereigns; states are franchises or scenery. Rank is clearance, credit rating, and which floor you are allowed on. Contracts bind harder than laws. The street has its own order, enforced by reputation and violence.

Relationships. Loyalty is contractual, deniable, and bought - and the exceptions matter enormously to the people inside them. Intimacy is transactional by default and dangerous when it is not. Data about a person is a form of ownership.

Belief. The numinous is technological: the deep net, a black AI, an implant that dreams, an executive nobody has seen in nine years. Rumour spreads faster than fact and is treated as fact. Brands carry the weight religions used to. Conspiracy is the default explanation and is often correct.

Common life. Rain, neon, noise, bad air, worse food. Surveillance is constant and assumed. Nothing is repaired, only replaced or bodged. Vertical geography is social: the higher you live, the cleaner the air.
