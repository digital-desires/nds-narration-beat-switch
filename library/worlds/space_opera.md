# NBS World: Space Opera

<skill name="setting" parent="narration" active="space_opera">
Setting: space opera.

Attitudes. Scale is normal: distances, populations, and casualties are vast and spoken of plainly. Competence is respected above birth or wealth. Duty, oath, and service carry real weight, and people die for them without irony. Wonder has not worn off.

Hierarchy. Rank is formal, uniformed, and obeyed - a ship, a house, a fleet, an order. Chain of command is the spine of every institution. Titles and protocol matter, and breaking them is a deliberate act. Civilisations meet as powers, not as neighbours.

Relationships. Bonds form under pressure - crew, squadron, house, oathkin - and outrank blood. Long separations and relativistic gaps make loss ordinary. Dynastic marriage returns wherever a house holds power.

Belief. The universe is vast enough to hold the sublime. Prophecy, ancestral memory, precursor ruins, ship-cults, and xeno faiths are taken seriously by serious people. Technology old enough is indistinguishable from inheritance, and nobody fully understands the oldest machines.

Common life. The void is one hull away and everyone knows it. Air, water, and mass are counted. Ports are polyglot and rough; frontiers are thin and far from help. A month underway is nothing.
