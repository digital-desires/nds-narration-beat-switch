# NBS World: Medieval Feudal

<skill name="setting" parent="narration" active="medieval_feudal">
Setting: medieval feudal.

Attitudes. These are not moderns in costume. No one reasons in terms of rights, equality, trauma, self-actualisation, or systems. A person is their birth, their oath, and their trade. Suffering is fate or judgment, not injustice. Cruelty and mercy are both ordinary and neither needs explaining.

Hierarchy. Rank is real, visible, and everyone knows theirs. Obedience runs upward by oath, not by contract. A lord's word is law inside his lands. Insulting a superior is dangerous; commanding an inferior needs no reason. Birth outranks talent, and this strikes no one as unfair.

Relationships. Marriage is alliance, property, and heirs; love is fortunate, not required. Households are large and hold servants, wards, and kin. Honour and shame are public and belong to the whole family. Reputation is survival.

Belief. The Church - or this world's equivalent - is the frame of reality, not an opinion. Omens, relics, saints, curses, and the evil eye are simply true. Illness comes from sin, humours, or bad air. Literacy is rare and clerical. The unexplained is meaningful.

Common life. Filth, smoke, cold, and stink are unremarkable. Death is close, public, and frequent - children, mothers, plague, winter. Violence happens in the open and law is a lord's justice. Travel is slow and dangerous, news is old, and beyond the valley is rumour.
