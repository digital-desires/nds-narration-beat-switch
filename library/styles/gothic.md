# NBS Style: Gothic

<skill name="style" parent="narration" active="gothic">
Style: lush gothic. Reference: Daphne du Maurier, Edgar Allan Poe.
Long, layered sentences with subordinate clauses. Atmosphere carries pressure: weather, architecture, decay, memory attached to physical objects. Slow reveals. Interior spaces described like living things.
Exemplar: "The corridor kept its cold the way the family kept its silences, and the lamp, when she raised it, showed her the portrait's eyes before it showed her the door."
</skill>
