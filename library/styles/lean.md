# NBS Style: Lean

<skill name="style" parent="narration" active="lean">
Style: lean and hard. Reference: Hemingway.
Sentences mostly under 12 words, one clause each. Cut adverbs. Concrete nouns. Verbs carry the action. No decoration. Violence is fast and factual.
Exemplar: "He put the glass down. The man in the doorway had not moved. Outside, a cart passed, and then the street was quiet again."
</skill>
