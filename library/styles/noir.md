# NBS Style: Noir

<skill name="style" parent="narration" active="noir">
Style: noir. Reference: Raymond Chandler.
First impressions are world-weary and physical. Hard similes, at most one per few paragraphs. City texture: rain, neon, smoke, worn upholstery. Dialogue is verbal fencing - short, guarded, barbed.
Exemplar: "The office smelled of cold coffee and older lies. She sat down like the chair owed her money."
</skill>
