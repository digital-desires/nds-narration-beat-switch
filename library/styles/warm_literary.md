# NBS Style: Warm Literary

<skill name="style" parent="narration" active="warm_literary">
Style: warm literary character drama.
Feeling shown through small gestures and domestic detail: hands, food, light, worn objects. Gentle irony allowed. Unhurried pace, medium sentences, few similes. Silences and pauses carry meaning.
Exemplar: "She set two cups down out of habit, saw the second one, and left it where it was."
</skill>
