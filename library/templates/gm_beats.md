# NBS Template: GM Beats

<capsule label="clash" trigger="Physical attacks are being exchanged now. Not anticipated violence.">
<moment>Force is being exchanged. Someone is trying to end this now.</moment>
<contest>Opposed. Attacker's weapon or unarmed skill against the defender's best active defence, and the defender picks one per attack: Dodge = Basic Speed + 3 dropping fractions, where Basic Speed = (HT + DX) / 4; Parry = weapon skill / 2 + 3; Block = shield skill / 2 + 3, both rounded down. Against someone who cannot defend — asleep, restrained, unaware — it is unopposed.</contest>
<modifiers>Reach and position decide more than strength. Out of reach or crowded, -2. Fighting from below, on bad footing, or off balance, -2. Bad light, -2 to -4; blind, -10. Each attacker past the first on one target, -1 to that target's defence. Striking a small area — hand, eye, weapon — is -4 to -8. All-out attack: +4 to hit, no defence until your next turn. All-out defence: +2 to one defence, no attack. Retreating a step as you defend, +3 Dodge or +1 Parry or Block, once per turn.</modifiers>
<margin>The gap is how and where it lands. A large gap is a solid hit somewhere that matters; a small one is a graze or a hit that catches armour; zero means it lands but costs the attacker position, a weapon, or an opening; negative means the defence held and the attacker is out of place.

Then subtract armour — DR 2-3 leather or heavy cloth, 4-5 mail, 6-7 plate, more for powered or fantastic protection. A hit that does not get past DR leaves a bruise and a scored surface, nothing more. That is why armoured opponents are hard; they do not dodge better.

A hit that gets through is a wound worth -2 to everything that character does afterwards, -4 if it was serious. Those are condition penalties. Stage 2 picks them up on the next exchange, so the second trade is not fought at the strength of the first.</margin>
<stake>A fight always has one. Minimum: position, a weapon, a way out. Normal: a wound that persists. High: a crippled limb, capture, or a death. Name it before writing and let it land at that size.</stake>
<reads>Concrete and physical. Where bodies are, what they did, what it cost. No numbers, no named defences. Fights are short — a few exchanges, then someone is down, fled, or holding a door.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="coil" trigger="Immediate danger is held: stealth, hiding, standoff, or pending ambush. Not active combat or flight.">
<moment>Danger is present and has not gone off. Stealth, a standoff, an ambush not yet sprung.</moment>
<contest>Opposed. Stealth against the watcher's Perception — Perception equals IQ unless the sheet says otherwise. For a standoff where nobody is hiding, it is Will against Will: who moves, speaks, or lowers a weapon first.</contest>
<modifiers>Moving at half speed, +2 to Stealth; standing still, +4. Running, -4. Darkness or heavy noise, +2 to +5. Hard floors, dry leaves, mail, or anything carried loose, -1 to -3. A watcher who is actively searching, +2 to their Perception; one who is bored or distracted, -2 to -4. Cover you are already behind counts; cover you would have to reach does not.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is time and knowledge, not safety. Well ahead means you are not noticed and you learn something — a count, a name, a route. Slightly ahead means unnoticed, but you learn nothing new. Zero means something registers: a head turns, a conversation stops, and you have one moment to act before it resolves. Behind means noticed, and the beat becomes clash or pursuit.</margin>
<stake>Position and surprise. Winning here usually means choosing when the next beat starts, which is worth more than any single hit.</stake>
<reads>Draw the interval out. Small sounds carry. Let the reader feel the length of the wait rather than being told it was tense.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="dread" trigger="External horror or oppression dominates while reality remains clear.">
<moment>Something is wrong, and it is outside. Perception is intact; the horror is real, not a distortion.</moment>
<contest>Unopposed, against Will — Will equals IQ unless stated. This is not a contest with the thing; it is whether the person holds together in front of it.</contest>
<modifiers>Set the difficulty by what is being faced: something merely unnerving, no penalty; a violated body or an impossible thing, -2; something that unmakes what the character believed was true, -4 or worse. Being armed, in company, or on familiar ground, +1 to +2. Alone, in the dark, cornered, or already hurt, -1 to -3. Someone who has faced this before is +2 the second time and immune to it by the fifth — horror wears out, and it should.</modifiers>
<margin>Ahead means they function: hands steady enough, voice under control, able to act this turn. Zero means they act but badly — a fumbled word, a step back, an order given too loud. Behind means fear takes the turn: frozen, retreating, or doing the wrong useful-looking thing. Well behind means it costs more than the turn — a scream that carries, dropping what they were holding, or a mark on them that later scenes remember.</margin>
<stake>Composure, and what breaking it costs in front of these particular witnesses. Occasionally the exit, if fear sends someone the wrong way.</stake>
<reads>Withhold. Show the edge of the thing and what it did to the people looking, never its whole shape.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="passage" trigger="Exploring, travelling through, or discovering a place is the focus.">
<moment>Moving through a place, or finding out what is in it. The place is the subject.</moment>
<contest>Unopposed against the relevant skill: Navigation to hold a course, Survival to live off the ground, Climbing at DX-5 if untrained, Swimming at HT-4 if untrained, Perception to notice what is not advertised. The place is difficulty, not an opponent — it is not trying to stop anyone.</contest>
<modifiers>Set difficulty from the ground: a road or a lit corridor, no penalty; broken country, ruins, or bad weather, -2; a cliff, a flooded passage, or a whiteout, -4 to -6. Proper gear — rope, lamp, boots, a map — is +1 to +3, and its absence is the same penalty in reverse. Haste, -2. Searching thoroughly rather than passing through, +2 but it costs time. Encumbrance is real: carrying more than ST squared over five pounds costs -1 to -4 on movement and on anything physical.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is distance, time, and what gets noticed. Well ahead means arriving sooner than expected and seeing something worth seeing. Ahead means arriving. Zero means arriving late, lost briefly, or a supply spent. Behind means the route fails — turned back, benighted, a twisted ankle. Well behind means real trouble: separated, without water, or somewhere nobody knows to look.</margin>
<stake>Time and supplies, mostly. Occasionally the route itself, when there is no second way through.</stake>
<reads>Specific and sensory, honest about scale. What it smells like. How long it actually takes.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="pursuit" trigger="Urgent movement: chase, escape, race, or deadline-driven travel.">
<moment>Someone is trying to reach or escape something, and time is against them.</moment>
<contest>Opposed, and repeated. Each exchange is Basic Move plus the relevant skill — Running, Riding, Driving — against the other side's. A chase does not end on one comparison: it ends when one side is ahead by enough to break line of sight, or when someone runs out of air.</contest>
<modifiers>Knowing the ground, +2 to +4, and this is usually the whole chase. Crowds, market stalls, and traffic, -2 to whoever is less willing to shove. Carrying anything heavy, -1 to -4 by encumbrance. Vaulting, climbing, or crossing water swaps the contest to that skill for one exchange. A pursuer with a mount, a vehicle, or a shortcut starts several points ahead and should not be caught by determination alone.</modifiers>
<margin>The gap moves the distance, and fatigue is the real clock. Every exchange after the second costs a point of endurance from both sides, and a runner who spends more than their HT in total is at -1 to everything, then -2, then stops. Ahead means the gap opens. Zero means it holds and both parties pay another point. Behind means the gap closes and the next exchange may be clash.</margin>
<stake>Escape or capture, and it should genuinely be able to go either way. A chase that cannot be lost is a travel scene.</stake>
<reads>Momentum in the prose. Short units. The gap opening or closing is the through-line, and the reader should always know which way it is going.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="heat" trigger="Sexual intimacy is beginning or under way.">
<moment>Intimacy has moved from wanting to happening.</moment>
<contest>Usually none. Where both parties want this, nothing resists and there is nothing to resolve — do not manufacture a contest out of a scene that has already been agreed to. Where one party is uncertain, it is their own weighing, not a skill anyone can beat: what they want against what it costs them.</contest>
<modifiers>Willingness is read live, from what people say and do in this moment, never assumed from an earlier one, and it can change mid-scene. Privacy, time, and safety each move a hesitant party toward yes; an audience, a deadline, or somewhere they might be found move them away. A tracked relationship value sets the register — familiar or careful, plain or unspoken. Injury and exhaustion do not suspend themselves for this: a wound is still -2 to anything physical attempted here, -4 if it was serious, and a body that is hurt behaves like one.</modifiers>
<margin>Where nothing resists there is no margin, and none is needed. Where someone is uncertain, what shifts is not success or failure but how far the scene goes and what is said afterwards.</margin>
<stake>What this changes between them, which is usually more than either intended.</stake>
<reads>The active style sub-skill owns explicitness and length in this beat without exception; follow it. With no style active, keep to gesture, response, and what changes between them, and let the scene close where it naturally closes.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="flirt" trigger="Romantic or seductive tension without sexual action now.">
<moment>Attraction is in play and nobody has committed to it.</moment>
<contest>Opposed. Sex Appeal or Savoir-Faire against the other person's Will. Note that Sex Appeal runs off HT, not IQ — presence and vitality, not cleverness — and it defaults to HT-3 for anyone untrained.</contest>
<modifiers>Whether they are inclined toward you at all is worth +3 to -3 before anything is said, and it is set by who they are, not by how good the line was. Being seen by people whose opinion matters, -2. Obvious ulterior motive, -2 to -4. Shared danger just survived, +1 to +2. Trying a second time in the same scene after a clear miss, -2, and a third time is not a roll, it is a refusal.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is interest and permission, one step at a time — attention, then warmth, then an invitation. It never buys the next beat outright. Zero means they are interested and say something that costs you: a question you would rather not answer, or a condition. Behind means the register cools and the subject changes. Well behind means it lands badly enough that other people notice.</margin>
<stake>Standing, mostly, and how this person treats you for the rest of the story.</stake>
<reads>Subtext. Nobody says the actual thing. Deflection, timing, and what a pause does.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="politics" trigger="Institutional power involving factions, policy, alliances, votes, or public influence.">
<moment>Power exercised through people and institutions — factions, alliances, policy, influence.</moment>
<contest>Opposed. Diplomacy against Will for persuasion, or against a rival's own Diplomacy where two people are working the same room. Diplomacy is a hard skill and defaults to IQ-6, so someone untrained is genuinely bad at this and should be.</contest>
<modifiers>What you are actually offering is the largest modifier here: something they need, +2 to +4; something they merely like, +1; nothing, -2. Rank and reputation, +1 to +3 either way. Asking them to act against their own faction's interest, -4, and no amount of charm removes it. A favour owed, +2. Being publicly on record afterwards, -2 — people commit less when it can be quoted.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is a position, not an outcome. Well ahead means they will act, and soon. Ahead means they agree in principle and will need reminding. Zero means agreement with a price attached — a condition, a favour called in later, or their name kept out of it. Behind means a polite no that closes the door for this session. Well behind means they now have a reason to work against you.</margin>
<stake>Standing that persists. Political capital is a resource and it depletes; spending it here means it is not available later.</stake>
<reads>People, not abstractions. Politics happens in rooms, between named people with something to lose.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="court" trigger="Rank, protocol, ceremony, oath, or ritual procedure is the obstacle.">
<moment>Rank, protocol, ceremony, oath, or ritual is the thing in the way.</moment>
<contest>Unopposed against Savoir-Faire, or the setting's equivalent body of correct form. It is an easy skill for anyone raised to it and defaults to IQ-4 for everyone else, which is the entire drama of an outsider at court. Where an official is actively obstructing rather than merely watching, it becomes opposed against their Will.</contest>
<modifiers>Belonging here by birth or office, +2 to +4. Visibly not belonging — wrong clothes, wrong accent, wrong weapon still on your hip, -2 to -4. Having been coached beforehand, +2. A ceremony with a fixed script, -2, because there is no room to improvise. Rank of the person being addressed: each step above you, -1.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is passage and standing. Well ahead means the form works for you and someone influential notices. Ahead means you get through. Zero means you get through and someone saw the error, which will be mentioned later. Behind means you are corrected in public. Well behind means the form is used against you — refused, delayed, or escorted out.</margin>
<stake>Reputation, in front of witnesses who will still be here next session. Occasionally access that cannot be regained.</stake>
<reads>Precise. The correct words in the correct order, and what it means when somebody gets one wrong.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="negotiation" trigger="Reaching agreement on terms, price, trade, concession, or exchange.">
<moment>Terms are being reached — price, trade, concession, exchange.</moment>
<contest>Opposed. Merchant against Merchant for a trade, or the relevant social skill against Will where what is being traded is not goods. Settle three things before writing the other party: what they want, what they will accept, and where they walk away.</contest>
<modifiers>Their alternatives are the real modifier: someone with a better offer elsewhere is +4 and does not need this deal. Urgency on your side, -2, and it is visible. Urgency on theirs, +2. Knowing the true value of the goods, +2. Being an obvious outsider, -2. A standing relationship, +1 to +3. Threats move this to intimidation and change what the beat is.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is terms, and terms are granular — price, timing, delivery, exclusivity, what happens if it goes wrong. Well ahead means most of what you asked. Ahead means the deal at a fair price. Zero means the deal with something attached you did not want. Behind means no deal at this price. Well behind means no deal at all, and they remember being pushed.</margin>
<stake>Money and goods, and whether this person deals with you again.</stake>
<reads>Concrete goods, numbers and terms. Offers, counters, silences. Let someone walk away when they should.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="probe" trigger="Extracting or testing information, testimony, or confession under pressure.">
<moment>Information is being extracted or tested — questioning, interrogation, cross-examination.</moment>
<contest>Opposed. Interrogation or Intimidation against the subject's Will. If the question is whether they are lying rather than whether they will talk, it is your Perception-based Detect Lies against their Fast-Talk or Acting — and Detect Lies is a hard skill, defaulting to Perception-6.</contest>
<modifiers>Before any of it: what do they actually know? Nobody reveals what they do not have, and no margin changes that. Evidence in hand, +2 to +4. Something they want in exchange, +2. Protecting someone they love, -4. Fear of what talking costs them later, -2 to -4. Physical coercion, +2 in the moment and -4 to everything they say afterwards, because frightened people say what stops the pain.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is how much and how true. Well ahead means a straight answer with detail. Ahead means the answer, bare. Zero means part of it, or the answer plus a condition. Behind means evasion, a half-truth, or a demand of their own. Well behind means a convincing lie you do not immediately catch, or silence that hardens.</margin>
<stake>What they do after — warn someone, comply, or hold a grudge you will meet later.</stake>
<reads>Only what is observable: words, choices, demeanour. What they know, and whether they are lying, stays yours until something in-world makes it public.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="dispute" trigger="Personal verbal conflict, accusation, argument, or clash of wills.">
<moment>A personal argument — accusation, grievance, or a clash of wills between people who know each other.</moment>
<contest>Opposed, Will against Will. Where one side is genuinely arguing rather than pressing, use their relevant social skill against the other's Will instead. Being right is not the contest; conviction and leverage are.</contest>
<modifiers>Evidence they cannot dismiss, +2 to +4. An old grievance underneath this one, -2 to whoever is carrying it, because they are arguing about something else. Being publicly observed, -1 to both, since neither can back down cleanly. Genuine authority over the other, +2. A tracked relationship that is low is why this is happening and is worth -2 to any attempt to settle it here.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is position, not agreement. Nobody concedes an argument cleanly. Well ahead means they stop pressing and something shifts in your favour later. Ahead means they drop it for now. Zero means it stays open and both of you said something that will be remembered. Behind means you give ground. Well behind means the relationship takes damage that a later scene has to pay for.</margin>
<stake>The relationship, and what each of them tells other people afterwards.</stake>
<reads>People talk past each other. Nobody concedes cleanly. What is not said carries.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="fracture" trigger="A false or distorted reality is perceived as fact.">
<moment>Someone holds a false picture of reality as fact — deceived, mistaken, or manipulated.</moment>
<contest>Opposed. To see through it: Perception-based Detect Lies, or the relevant knowledge skill, against the deceiver's Fast-Talk or Acting. To correct someone else's false picture: your social skill against their Will, and Will is doing real work here because people defend a wrong picture hardest when abandoning it costs them.</contest>
<modifiers>A detail that does not fit and is physically present, +3 — evidence beats argument. Being told by someone they trust, +2; by someone they do not, -2. The belief explaining something painful for them, -4. Having said it out loud in public already, -2, because now retracting costs face. Time to check independently, +2.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is doubt, not truth. Well ahead means they see it and act on it. Ahead means they doubt and go looking. Zero means they doubt and say nothing, which changes what they do without changing what they say. Behind means the belief holds and they explain the inconsistency away. Well behind means they conclude you are the one lying to them.</margin>
<stake>What they do while still wrong, which is usually the real danger in this beat.</stake>
<reads>Write the false picture from inside it, with no narrator correction. The gap shows through what does not fit.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="haze" trigger="Reality feels soft or uncertain through dreams, intoxication, hypnosis, or dissociation.">
<moment>Perception itself is unreliable — dream, intoxication, hypnosis, dissociation, fever.</moment>
<contest>Two layers, and you settle the underneath one first. What is actually happening resolves normally, by whatever contest that situation calls for. Then the impairment is an unopposed HT or Will roll to act through it — HT for something physical or chemical, Will for something imposed on the mind.</contest>
<modifiers>The impairment is a flat penalty to everything the affected person attempts: mild, -1 or -2; badly drunk, drugged, or half-asleep, -3 or -4; barely conscious, -6 and no fine work at all. Familiar routine, +2, because the body knows it. Anything requiring judgement or new information, an extra -2. Cold water, pain, or a shout is +2 for one exchange and nothing after.</modifiers>
<margin>The underneath layer resolves as it resolves, and the impairment penalty is already inside the numbers — do not apply it twice. What the gap buys on the impairment itself is clarity: ahead means a lucid stretch where they can be relied on, zero means they act but misread something, behind means they act on the distortion as though it were real.</margin>
<stake>What gets decided or said while judgement is gone, and who was there to hear it.</stake>
<reads>Distort continuity, sensory register and time. Never distort the reader's ability to follow. Something real must survive the scene.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="awe" trigger="Vast, sacred, cosmic, or sublime scale creates overwhelming wonder.">
<moment>Scale — sacred, cosmic, vast, ancient — larger than the people looking at it.</moment>
<contest>Usually none, and that is the correct ruling. Nothing is being attempted and nothing resists. Where the scale is genuinely overwhelming rather than merely large, it is an unopposed Will roll to keep functioning — the same procedure as dread, pointed at wonder instead of fear.</contest>
<modifiers>Where a roll is called for at all: something merely enormous, no penalty; something that contradicts what the character believed the world contained, -2 to -4. Religious or professional framing that gives it a name and a place, +2 — people who have a word for it cope better. Alone, -1.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is composure and attention. Ahead means they take it in and can still speak, act, and notice detail. Zero means they lose the thread of what they were doing. Behind means they stop entirely and something happens around them that they miss.</margin>
<stake>Usually nothing mechanical. Occasionally the thing they failed to notice while staring.</stake>
<reads>Concrete detail at the wrong scale. One accurate physical fact beats any adjective. Let people be small and say small things.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="machina" trigger="A skilled technical procedure under meaningful constraint.">
<moment>A technical procedure under real constraint — repair, intrusion, ritual, surgery, sabotage.</moment>
<contest>Unopposed against the specific skill, unless someone built this to resist exactly this attempt, in which case it is opposed against the builder's own skill. Most technical skills default badly or not at all: guessing at a system you have never trained on is -6 at best, and for many things it is simply impossible rather than difficult.</contest>
<modifiers>The right tools, +2 to +4; improvised tools, -2 to -5; no tools, often impossible. Time is the largest lever: doubling the time taken is +2, quadrupling it +4, and halving it -2, down to -10 for something done in a single desperate second. Good light and a stable surface, +1 to +2. Working from documentation or a known-good example, +2. Having done this exact thing before, +1.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is quality and permanence. Well ahead means it works and keeps working. Ahead means it works. Zero means it works for now — a field repair that will fail at the worst moment, a bypass that logs an alert. Behind means it does not work and you have used the time. Well behind means the thing is worse than before: a snapped part, a triggered alarm, a patient bleeding.</margin>
<stake>Time and materials first. Then whatever depends on the thing working.</stake>
<reads>The right nouns. Name components and steps. Competence reads through specificity.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="ember" trigger="Quiet care, comfort, bonding, or non-sexual intimacy.">
<moment>Quiet care between people — comfort, tending, company, closeness without sex.</moment>
<contest>None, and do not invent one. Nothing is being extracted and nobody is being persuaded. Where someone is being physically tended, First Aid or the setting's equivalent applies as an ordinary unopposed skill, and a success restores a little of what was lost rather than all of it.</contest>
<modifiers>For tending: proper supplies, +2; improvised, -2; treating someone who will not stay still, -2. For everything else, no modifiers, because there is no roll — what governs is what the relationship already is and what each person can accept right now.</modifiers>
<margin>Only the tending has a margin, and what it buys is recovery: a wound moving from serious to manageable, a condition penalty easing by a point, someone able to walk. The rest of the beat has no margin because it has no contest. What it has instead is a small, real move in the tracked relationship.</margin>
<stake>Low, and that is the point. What is at risk is only being refused, which is its own kind of cost.</stake>
<reads>Small physical detail. Hands, food, warmth, worn objects. Silence is dialogue here.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="wound" trigger="Grief, shame, heartbreak, despair, or emotional collapse dominates.">
<moment>Grief, shame, heartbreak, despair. The feeling is the event.</moment>
<contest>Unopposed against Will, and only when they are trying to do something in spite of it. There is no contest against grief itself and no margin that resolves it — it is a condition, not an obstacle.</contest>
<modifiers>The state is worth -2 to everything while it is acute, -4 in the first hours of something severe, and it does not clear inside one scene. Being needed by someone right now, +2, because people function for others when they cannot for themselves. Being watched by someone whose opinion they care about, -2. Alcohol or exhaustion, a further -2. This penalty is condition and stage 2 will keep applying it in later beats until something in the fiction eases it.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is only whether they can act at all this turn. Ahead means they hold it together and do the thing. Zero means they do it badly or say something they will regret. Behind means they cannot, and someone else has to.</margin>
<stake>What is left undone while they cannot act, and who sees them like this.</stake>
<reads>Understate. The body carries it, not the adjectives. Do not resolve it inside one beat.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="ledger" trigger="Routine planning, shopping, inventory, downtime, scheduling, or travel preparation.">
<moment>The unglamorous business — supplies, money, planning, repairs, scheduling, preparation.</moment>
<contest>Unopposed. Merchant to find a fair price, Area Knowledge to know who stocks it, the relevant craft skill to make or mend it. Most of this beat is not a contest at all but a lookup: what is in stock, what it costs, how long it takes.</contest>
<modifiers>A large town or port, +2 to finding anything; a village, -4, and some things are simply not available at any price. Wanting it today rather than next week, -2. Buying something restricted, illegal, or above the local technology, -4 or impossible. Haggling well, +1 to +2 on price. Reputation as a good customer, +1.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is price, quality, and time. Well ahead means found, cheap, and today. Ahead means found at a fair price. Zero means found but expensive, slow, or not quite what was wanted. Behind means not here — try elsewhere, or wait. Well behind means not here, and asking has been noticed by someone.</margin>
<stake>Money and time, which are the stakes that make later beats harder. Read tracked inventory and funds, and keep the fiction consistent with them.</stake>
<reads>Compress without skipping. Name specific goods and prices. What was decided here should still be findable later.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="levity" trigger="Comedy, playful banter, teasing, or genuine respite.">
<moment>Comedy, banter, teasing, or real rest. Nothing is at stake, deliberately.</moment>
<contest>None. Do not roll for a joke. Where someone is performing rather than simply being funny, the relevant skill applies as an ordinary unopposed check, and failing it is embarrassing rather than costly.</contest>
<modifiers>Not applicable to the beat itself. What matters is rest: 1 hour of genuine downtime returns 1 point of endurance, a full night returns all of it and eases a condition penalty by 1 step. Somewhere safe and warm, faster; on watch in the cold, not at all.</modifiers>
<margin>No margin, because there is no contest. What changes is condition — endurance back, tension down — and a small real move in how these people regard each other.</margin>
<stake>None. Do not introduce a threat to end the lull, and do not cut it short for momentum. A scene that changes only how people feel about each other has still changed something.</stake>
<reads>Let jokes land without explaining them. Timing over cleverness. Different characters find different things funny.</reads>
</capsule>

<capsule label="generic" trigger="Anything ordinary, mixed, unclear, or without one clearly dominant specialty.">
<moment>Ordinary, mixed, or unclear. No one specialty dominates.</moment>
<contest>Choose the closest skill or attribute yourself and run the standard engine. Opposed if a person or creature is actively resisting; unopposed if the difficulty is the situation itself. Where no skill fits, use the governing attribute: ST for force, DX for precision, IQ for knowledge and judgement, HT for endurance, Will for holding out, Perception for noticing.</contest>
<modifiers>Set them from what is actually present. A useful rule of thumb: something that would help a real person by a little is +1 or +2, by a lot is +3 or +4; the same in reverse for hindrances, and -5 and beyond where conditions make the attempt nearly hopeless. State whichever ones you applied.</modifiers>
<margin>What the gap buys is simply more or less of what was attempted — faster, cleaner, more complete, or the reverse.</margin>
<stake>Whatever the fiction already has at risk. If nothing is, say so, and let the attempt succeed without ceremony.</stake>
<reads>Follow the active style, or the default craft. Cover one beat and stop.</reads>
</capsule>
